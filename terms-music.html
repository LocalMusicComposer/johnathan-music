MUSIC TERMS

Payment

Two parts: an initial payment when I give you the final agreed price, and a final payment when I deliver the finished file (WAV or MP3). Work does not begin until the first payment is received. Accepted: PayPal, CashApp, Venmo.

Revisions

Each song includes three free revisions. Small changes, like a single chord, are free. Larger changes, like a whole verse, may cost extra.

Refunds

Refunds must be requested before composing begins. After composing has begun, refunds are denied.

Delivery

Anywhere from a few hours to about a week, depending on length and complexity. SFX are usually faster.

Before commissioning

  • Give a clear description of the song or sound.
  • Bring a tempo, style, length and max budget.
  • Say up front if it is for commercial use.
  • Anything beyond the agreed scope will be discussed first.

Rights

I may use your commission in advertisements and as a sample for future commissions. You may show it to friends with credit. Public use, such as stream sounds or edits, needs a commercial license. If you claim my work as your own and I find out, you will be reported and I will not compose for you again.
